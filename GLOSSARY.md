# Glossary

## Globe

A user's personal geographic collection of visited places. One account owns one globe in the MVP.

_Avoid_: trip, map, album when referring to the entire collection.

## Place

A city or town in a globe, shared by its repeat visits.

_Avoid_: visit, destination when the meaning is the persistent city record.

## Visit

One recorded occurrence of being at a place, with an optional date or date range and zero or more photos.

_Avoid_: trip, journey.

## Photo

A photograph belonging to one visit, with an optional caption.

_Avoid_: upload when referring to ready album content.

## Cover photo

The photograph selected to represent a place on the globe and place list.

## Published globe

A globe the owner has explicitly made accessible to visitors through its profile address.

## Visitor

A person viewing a published globe without needing an account.
