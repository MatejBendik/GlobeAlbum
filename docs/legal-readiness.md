# Privacy and terms for the public beta

Reviewed on 2026-11-07. The owner confirmed Matej Bendík as the individual
operator in Slovakia and the public portfolio mailbox for privacy and support.

The application serves `/privacy` and `/terms` without a sign-in requirement.
The footer links to both and to the content reporting instructions. The
sign-in and registration screens surface the terms and privacy notice before
either registration route. The footer's portfolio link carries a static
source, medium and campaign. The application does not collect link clicks.

The notices describe the managed account, public globe fields, media
processing, optional third-party requests, cookies and theme storage,
deletion and the data rights contact. The Google avatar comes from the
existing verified session and identifies the signed-in account in its own
menu, settings and studio. The browser fetches the avatar without a referrer.
GlobeAlbum does not write the avatar URL into its application user record or
include it in an anonymous public globe response. Since 2026-11-08, Settings
offers an explicit choice to save a processed copy of the Google photo. An
in-product disclosure explains public visibility and links to the updated
notice. The selected photo, whether imported or uploaded, appears beside the
owner's name while the globe is published and can be removed in Settings.

The [Google API Services User Data Policy](https://developers.google.com/terms/api-services-user-data-policy)
was verified on 2026-11-08 for the disclosure and consent before this additional use.

Before opening the public beta, the operator should review the following with
a Slovak privacy professional as needed for the intended audience and service.

- Verify processor terms and applicable transfer safeguards for the actual
  Vercel, Neon and Cloudflare accounts. Confirm the backup, request log and
  support retention criteria and the handling of data access and portability
  requests. Assess and record the necessity and retention of the deletion
  fence and retired usernames, which currently have no configured expiry.
- Review whether the service falls under the DSA or Slovak provider disclosure
  rules, including any required public contact or address, notice intake,
  acknowledgments and moderation decisions. The current reporting channel
  opens a structured email. It does not have an automated intake or appeals
  system. Confirm terms presentation and language for the target audience.
- Verify Google brand and audience on the owned domain, using the public
  privacy and terms URLs and the [OAuth setup](google-oauth.md).
